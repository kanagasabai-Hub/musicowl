# MusicOwl

A fast, offline music player for Windows, for the music files on your PC.

**[Download the latest release](https://github.com/kanagasabai-Hub/musicowl/releases/latest)**

## Features

- **Your library, organised:** songs, albums, artists, genres and folders, with instant search.
- **Made for you:** Daily Mixes, On Repeat, Rediscover and Hidden Gems, built from what you
  play, finish, skip and like, and from how your music actually sounds.
- **Radio:** start a radio from any song or artist; autoplay keeps similar music going when
  your queue ends.
- **Playlists and Liked Songs**, with suggestions of songs to add.
- **Multiple queues:** keep several listening sessions side by side.
- **Great sound:** gapless playback, optional crossfade and a 10-band equalizer with presets.
- **Lyrics:** synced (LRC) and plain lyrics embedded in your files.
- **Tag editor** for titles, artists, albums and more.
- **Windows integration:** media keys and the Windows media overlay, output device choice
  (follows your default device, including Bluetooth headphones), sleep timer.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- Supported formats: MP3, FLAC, AAC / M4A, ALAC, Ogg Vorbis, WAV and AIFF

## Getting started

1. Download `musicowl.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/kanagasabai-Hub/musicowl/releases/latest).
2. Run it and choose **Add music folder**.
3. Press play. Personal mixes appear as MusicOwl learns what you like.

## Privacy

MusicOwl works entirely offline: it makes no network connections, and your library,
listening history and settings never leave your PC. They are stored in
`%APPDATA%\MusicOwl`; delete that folder to reset the app.
